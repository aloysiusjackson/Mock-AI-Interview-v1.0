Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Is Interview.AI free to use? +
Yes! Interview.AI offers a free tier with generous practice sessions. You can start practicing immediately after signing up — no credit card required.
How does the AI interview work? +
Our AI asks realistic interview questions based on your selected role and industry. You respond using your microphone, and the AI provides instant feedback on your clarity, confidence, structure, and knowledge.
Can I practice for specific companies? +
Yes! You can select your target role and the AI will tailor questions to that specific domain. Whether it's technical coding interviews, behavioral questions, or HR rounds — we've got you covered.
Do I need a microphone? +
For the best experience, we recommend using a microphone. However, you can also type your responses. The AI feedback works with both voice and text inputs.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your practice sessions are stored so we can show your progress and generate feedback, and they are never sold or shared with third parties. Sessions are not end-to-end encrypted — see our Privacy Policy for the full details.
