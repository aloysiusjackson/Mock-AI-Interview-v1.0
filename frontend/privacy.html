Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it — written to actually be readable.

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

Interview.AI is a mock-interview practice tool. We only collect what the product needs to work:

  • Account details — your name and email address, supplied when you register or when you sign in with Google.
  • A password — stored only as a salted hash. Your actual password is never saved in readable form.
  • Your practice data — interview sessions, answer transcripts, scores, and the feedback generated from them. This is what powers your dashboard and progress charts.
  • Your resume — only if you choose to upload one to get tailored questions. It is used to generate questions and is not published anywhere.
  • Your preferences — target role and light/dark theme.

2. What we do not collect

  • No payment details — the product does not process payments.
  • No advertising or marketing trackers.
  • No precise location, contacts, or microphone audio sent to us — recording happens in your browser for live transcription only.

3. Where your data lives

Two places:

  • On our server — in the application database, for your account and your practice history. This is what lets you log in from another device and still see your progress.
  • In your browser — as localStorage entries such as user, app-theme, and welcome-dismissed. These keep you signed in between visits and remember your theme. Clearing your browser data removes them; it does not delete your account.

Honest note: this is a practice application, not a certified medical or financial system. Data is stored in a standard application database and is not end-to-end encrypted.

4. How your data is used

  • To create your account and let you sign in.
  • To score your answers and show your progress, averages, and skill level.
  • To send transactional email — account verification links and password-reset links. Nothing else. We do not send marketing email.
  • To detect tab-switching during a session, so practice scores stay meaningful. This is a page-visibility check, not screen capture.

5. Third parties

  • Google Fonts — the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface is loaded from Google's CDN, which sees your IP address like any web request.
  • Chart.js — a charting library served from jsDelivr's CDN.
  • Google Sign-In — used only if you choose it. Google shares your name, email, and profile picture with us so we can create your account.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers.

6. How long we keep it

Until you ask us to remove it. Your account and practice history are retained so your progress remains available to you.

7. Your choices

  • You can clear the browser storage at any time to remove the local session and preferences.
  • You can request deletion of your account and everything attached to it by emailing us — see below. We will confirm when it is done.
  • You can decline to use Google Sign-In and register with an email address instead.

8. Children

The service is aimed at job seekers and students preparing for interviews. If you are under the age of digital consent in your region, please use it with a parent or guardian's permission.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way we handle data changes, this page is updated with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use after a change means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or concerns about your data: email aloysiusjackson39@gmail.com or use the contact page.
